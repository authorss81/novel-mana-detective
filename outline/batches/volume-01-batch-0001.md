# Volume 01 Batch 0001 Outline — Chapters 1–10

**Batch range:** Chapters 1–10.
**Volume arc:** Arc A, "The Missing Release," of the five arcs in `../volume-01.md`.
**Batch goal:** Establish Elias's ordinary work, the Veyra setting, the Sequence Sight rule, and the first concrete copywork case; give the team a small victory and a real cost while making the copied release impossible to ignore. Close the local promise — *how Tamsin Ro died, and how the copy was made* — and hand a larger pressure to the next batch.
**Expected length:** 10 chapters, roughly 24,000–30,000 words. Do not pad to reach it.

## Volume placement

This batch owns escalation beats 1–4 and opens beat 5. It does **not** own the volume climax, the certificate, the omitted witness, the reprimand, the dismantled workshop, or the escaped source anchor. Those belong to Arc E, Chapters 41–50, batch 0005. Ending Chapters 1–10 on a clinic that is "closed but not resolved" would spend the volume on its first page.

## Starting condition

Elias Venn is a Sequence Desk detective sergeant with an inactive legal file on the Morrow Street ward collapse, a damaged left ear, and no public authority for the Margin faculty. Nadia Kest is his police partner. Mara Quill is a skeptical forensic residue mage attached to Saint Orra Reach. A Kettle Rows night-bus dispatcher, Tamsin Ro, has been found dead in a rented room **two floors above Elias's own apartment**, in the same building whose ground floor is his repair shop.

Elias is on the case because he is the nearest Sequence Desk sergeant and he lives on the site, not because he asked for it. The conflict of interest is a running condition of the batch, not a single scene: the Concord inspector raises it in Chapter 2, Nadia records it in Chapter 3, and the Proof Registry uses it in Chapter 9 to make him easier to overrule. The building's owner has a licensed ward certificate that a dangerous-working finding would endanger, which gives everyone around the case a reason to want a boring answer.

## Starting relationships

- Elias and Nadia work well together but disagree about whether a raw reading can justify an emergency action.
- Elias and Mara have not yet agreed on consent, chain of custody, or what Elias's ability counts as evidence.
- Sana Okafor and the Kettle Rows clinic trust patients more than official certificates, but they do not yet trust Elias with a living trace.
- Jules Ardent is absent from the opening and enters as a mechanical suspect, not an ally.

## Starting power level

**First Margin:** Elias can perceive a fresh working's causal order after contact. He cannot read motive, identity, or a living person's inner trace; he cannot interrupt a working. He also cannot tell copywork from a genuine sequence by himself: he can see a release standing where someone's own working has none, and he can notice the same act recurring in two places, but the word *copy* and the seam belong to Mara and her instruments. The first reading will cost him time and physical stability.

## Batch midpoint

**Chapter 5.** By then Elias has identified a physical **carrier** — a portable ward clamp, not the source object — and traced it through three shops to a legal rehabilitation clinic whose intake records name no patient who was ever treated. The witness's account still contradicts the residue. Elias has a small victory and a serious doubt: the tool everyone wanted to arrest him over cannot be the thing that killed Tamsin Ro, and a trained memory can make a sequence look false or true without anyone lying.

Do not promise a rescued victim or an identified source object at the midpoint. The release pattern is named in Chapter 9 and the frame is broken in Chapter 10; the source object itself stays unidentified, because nobody has reached the network yet.

## Batch climax

**Chapter 10.** A copywork crew returns to the Kettle Rows clinic at night to remove a second carrier — one built for the clinic's own ward-light circuit and attached to Niko's transfer order — along with its template, before the dispensary's records can be copied. The clamp from Chapter 5 is contaminated and in Mara's lab at Saint Orra Reach, so this frame is something Elias has never held. It starts a small working on the ward circuit. Elias has no counterturn and no safe reading, so he uses ordinary police procedure, Jules's knowledge of the physical route, and Mara's measurement to identify where the carrier is bound. The working stops when the carrier is physically broken, not because Elias defeats a practitioner. No patient is harmed, the operator escapes, the network learns that Elias can read a working, and the Registry's certification order now stands against a file with no admissible answer. What the crew takes is a measurement, not a tool, which is the first sign that the frames are being made in series by someone with better materials than Jules.

## Naming and location decisions

- **Tamsin Ro** is the first victim. **Niko Senn** is the intended target, the witness, and the patient at risk; name him whenever he is present, intended, or discussed — Chapters 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10 — and never replace him with an unnamed "patient."
- **Orin Sable is not named in this batch.** The crew in the stockroom is anonymous, a set of hands and a borrowed clinic account. The question the batch ends on is *who is teaching the network*; naming the first frame-maker is Arc B's reward.
- The first crime scene is in Elias's own building, two floors above his apartment. Do not relocate it to a neutral witness site.

## Required character changes

- Elias moves from confident private reading toward accepting that Mara's measurements and a witness's disagreement are necessary evidence.
- Nadia records the conflict of interest and keeps Elias on the case on the condition that he stops working alone.
- Mara sets a consent boundary and treats Elias as a colleague with a dangerous limitation, not a useful magical shortcut.
- Jules changes from suspect to cautious technical ally after he returns the borrowed tools, records his unregistered repairs, and accepts responsibility for what he did.
- Sana's clinic becomes a moral and practical stakeholder rather than a location to be raided.
- The network learns that Elias reads workings, and someone begins treating that as a fact to plan around.

## Required power changes

- Elias takes four readings across the batch: one in the first minutes at the scene (Chapter 1), one controlled and logged (Chapter 3), one impulsive and unlogged that costs him the sample (Chapter 6), and one under load during the working (Chapter 10). Do not charge him the same symptoms twice; vary what each reading takes from him.
- The reading reveals a release standing where the victim never performed one, but not identity or intent, and not the fact that the working was copied — that diagnosis is Mara's measurement and the seam she finds.
- The readings cost Elias dizziness, nausea, lost minutes, and a brief nosebleed.
- No new active spell is learned. The temptation to act is stronger than his current ability.

## Required world reveals

- Magic is embedded in modern work: a bus depot ward, a clinic residue lab, a door working, and a certified sequence record.
- A working follows a causal order, not a video replay.
- Copywork needs a source anchor and a carrier. A carrier can hold a stolen release template; it is never the source object itself, and the first network's crude frame cannot yet insert a correction independently.
- A witness's remembered order can be trained, so the Margin can be fooled.
- A certificate is a document with chosen words, not a summary of the room. The Registry's public wording calls the incident a contained accident involving an unregistered ward tool; the draft certificate text records a complete self-directed working by the victim, with no third-party address and no contradicting witness. Elias is asked to countersign a document whose facts were decided before the evidence was finished.

## Out of scope for this batch

Do not deliver any of the following. Each belongs to a later arc and delivering it early flattens the volume:

- a stopped working with a living person as the live anchor;
- a signed, issued, or certified **dangerous-working certificate that closes the Kettle Rows case**, or the deletion of a contradicting witness from a record that exists. Niko's transfer paperwork and the rehabilitation certificate naming a treatment he never received are already in circulation and may be shown; Chapter 9 shows only a draft whose wording someone has already chosen, nothing is certified, no witness loses standing, and no case is closed;
- the arrest or exposure of Orin Sable, or the naming of the frame-maker;
- the frame workshop in The Cut, its dismantling, or the Aegis Mutual link;
- the source anchor leaving Veyra;
- Elias's formal reprimand, any loss of his post, and the loss of access to the samples from the first scene. Chapter 6 costs him the clamp and his standing with Mara, not his job;
- the volume's final image of the salt-glass plate wrapped in red thread.

The batch ends with an open file, a frightened patient who is safe for now, and a certification deadline.

## Open questions after the batch

- Who supplied the stolen release template, and who is teaching a crew to move a working through a living patient?
- Why does the copied release carry the same fabricated fourth act as the Morrow Street report?
- Who rehearsed Niko Senn, and what did the person who did it call the rehearsal at the time?
- Why was the draft certificate written as a self-directed working before the residue was measured?
- Can Mara's instruments measure the seam without touching a living patient?
- Who is paying for the legal rehabilitation clinic that has no patient in its intake records?

## Chapter Cards

### Chapter 0001 — The Door That Opened Twice

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** Kettle Rows, the rented room two floors above his own apartment, just after midnight.
**Immediate goal:** Secure the scene before the Concord inspector arrives and determine whether Tamsin Ro's death is an ordinary trespass or a magical working.
**Resistance:** The room's door ward has failed in a way that makes the physical lock and the magical threshold disagree; Elias is also carrying an old, unlogged memory of a similar door in the Morrow Street file.
**Information revealed:** Tamsin used a small return-home door working; the room has two different residue orders in its threshold.
**Action or decision:** Elias stops the first responders from moving the door, records the room before touching the frame, and calls Nadia rather than relying on the old case memory.
**Emotional change:** The case feels familiar in a way that makes him distrust his own first reaction, but asking Nadia for help costs him the fantasy of controlling the scene.
**Power or resource change:** His first deliberate Sequence Sight contact leaves him dizzy and missing four minutes; he has one unstable reading and no certificate.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** Tamsin Ro is the first victim; her death is linked to a copied working, not a self-directed attack. The red thread knotted around the old Morrow Street file stays where it is, and it is the reason he cannot stop looking at that door. The scene is in his own building, and everyone who finds out will ask what he was doing there.
**Ending type:** discovery.
**Next-chapter pull:** The door's residue does not match the room's physical lock, and someone on the street is watching a police van arrive.

### Chapter 0002 — A Room With Two Orders

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** The same rented room, after the first responders establish a perimeter.
**Immediate goal:** Separate ordinary physical evidence from magical residue and keep the Concord inspector from sealing the room before Mara arrives.
**Resistance:** The victim's personal belongings suggest a fight, while the residue suggests a working that began after the door opened; the apparent witnesses give incompatible times. The inspector also asks, in writing, whether the assigned detective lives at the address.
**Information revealed:** The sequence binds itself to something inside the room before it touches anything else, and the release comes after the working's turn but before the intended harm became physical — a release Tamsin never performed. The victim's last working was not interrupted; it was completed by an act nobody in the room did. Elias can see the order and the gap in it. He cannot yet say the word *copy*.
**Action or decision:** Elias photographs the physical scene, leaves the threshold untouched, and asks Mara to bring a salt-glass plate and an independent witness log. She lifts the threshold residue onto the plate in front of him before the inspector can order the room sealed. He answers the inspector's question about his residency on the record instead of dodging it.
**Emotional change:** He recognizes the missing release from a phrase in the Morrow Street file but refuses to let the old case dictate the new evidence; being the man who lives upstairs turns every piece of his care into something that can be attacked.
**Power or resource change:** He chooses not to take a second reading while the room is unstable; this costs him a chance to solve the case quickly and protects the sample.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** The sequence is causal order. Elias can see an order but not the motive behind it. The conflict of interest is now documented rather than deniable.
**Ending type:** cost.
**Next-chapter pull:** Mara finds a thin seam in the residue that matches neither the victim nor the door.

### Chapter 0003 — The Margin

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** Temporary evidence room at the Sequence Desk, late morning.
**Immediate goal:** Determine whether the seam is an old repair, a copywork frame, or a false witness before the Proof Registry asks him to sign a standard certificate.
**Resistance:** The residue is too fresh for a reliable comparison, Elias's left ear rings when he enters the room, and Nadia makes him log his own residency in the case file.
**Information revealed:** Elias's faculty is not a vision of people; it is a short perception of the order in which residue changed. The missing release is real and physically supported, and Mara's instruments put a seam in it that belongs neither to the victim nor to the door. Elias can see the absent act; only a measurement can say the act was assembled rather than performed. That is the ceiling of what he knows for the rest of the batch.
**Action or decision:** He accepts a limited, logged reading and records the exact moment it starts and ends. A single panel-like margin note appears: **MARGIN — A release follows the turn. A second trace begins before it.** The note makes him choose not to force a living-trace reading; he waits for Mara and the witness to set the next boundary.
**Emotional change:** The gift feels less like a gift and more like a responsibility he cannot delegate, and he is no longer the only person who knows what happened in that room.
**Power or resource change:** The reading takes his balance and then his memory: a nosebleed in a stairwell, and a block of the afternoon he has to rebuild from a phone log. Elias cannot touch a living person after it.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** The Margin gives order and omissions, never intent, identity, or legal proof. It cannot tell copywork from a genuine sequence; Mara's instruments can, and that division of labour holds for the whole batch.
**Ending type:** discovery.
**Next-chapter pull:** The only living trace that remains belongs to the intended target, who is refusing to speak to police.

### Chapter 0004 — The Witness Who Was There

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** Saint Orra Reach, a locked interview room with Mara and the intended target, **Niko Senn**.
**Immediate goal:** Obtain a safe account of what happened without asking Niko to submit to a second working or a coercive memory test.
**Resistance:** Niko remembers opening the door, but the order of the room's sounds is wrong; Mara insists that Elias stop asking leading questions.
**Information revealed:** Niko was made to rehearse a short sequence before the attack, possibly under pain or a disguised clinical instruction. A false rehearsal can put a different order in an inner trace.
**Action or decision:** Elias stops the interview, gives Niko the choice to speak without a recording, and lets Mara explain the consent boundary in plain language.
**Emotional change:** Niko's uncertainty frustrates Elias, but he recognizes that treating frustration as evidence of deception would be another form of control.
**Power or resource change:** Elias does not read Niko's living trace. He loses the fastest route to a resolution and gains a witness who will agree to independent care.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** False rehearsal alters remembered order but cannot create a physical injury by itself. Niko Senn is the intended target of the first copywork case and must be named as such from here on.
**Ending type:** decision.
**Next-chapter pull:** Niko remembers a brass tool marked with Jules Ardent's repair number.

### Chapter 0005 — The Small Repair

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** Kettle Rows repair shops and a hardware archive, afternoon.
**Immediate goal:** Trace the borrowed tool to whoever received it before the evidence is buried in three shops' transfer ledgers, and learn what the tool can physically do.
**Resistance:** The tool is ordinary, widely used, and missing from Jules's workshop; the operator has left a maintenance ticket that makes Jules look responsible. The archive clerk wants a warrant, and Elias is not the warrant.
**Information revealed:** Jules Ardent built a portable ward clamp, not a working frame. He admits lending the clamp to a clinic account under a false name for an unregistered repair; someone has since used its tooth pattern to carry Tamsin's copied release. The clamp cannot be the source object — it is a carrier, built to hold someone else's order.
**Action or decision:** Elias follows the repair number through three shops, records each transfer, and brings the tool to Mara without taking it into police custody.
**Emotional change:** He feels the first clean satisfaction of the case, then realizes the satisfaction came from treating Jules as a source rather than a person.
**Power or resource change:** The physical clamp gives him a second connected anchor, but he has not gained a new power stage.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** Jules is a ward mechanic who can explain physical anchors but cannot read sequences. A carrier is not a source object; the distinction is the midpoint's real content.
**Ending type:** discovery.
**Next-chapter pull:** The clamp was delivered to a legal rehabilitation clinic that has no patient matching its intake records.

### Chapter 0006 — What Elias Touched

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** Saint Orra Reach, Mara's residue laboratory, evening.
**Immediate goal:** Compare the clamp against Mara's plate of the threshold residue before the Registry can reclassify the clinic's ward fixtures as ordinary maintenance objects and seal what is left of the first scene.
**Resistance:** The Registry's preservation order will let an inspector treat the dispensary's stock as ordinary maintenance by morning, and Elias believes everything in the clinic will disappear into that order if he waits for a hearing. The clamp is in someone else's lab, which is the only reason he can touch it, and Nadia is two wards away holding a man who will not stop talking — so he is alone, which is the one thing she told him not to be.
**Information revealed:** The clamp carries the same release that stood in the room, and the order on it fails in exactly the same place — the same act, attempted twice, breaking at the same joint. Elias can see that it is not a new working. He cannot see who copied it or from what.
**Action or decision:** Elias touches the clamp on Mara's bench without logging the contact and gets a second, overwhelming reading. The sample becomes contaminated by a gesture that does not belong to the working.
**Emotional change:** He recognizes his own compulsion in a procedural failure. Mara refuses to use the contaminated reading, and the first useful evidence nearly disappears because of him.
**Power or resource change:** Elias gains a clearer sense of the sequence and loses physical stability, confidence, and any right to handle the clamp. He shakes for two hours and cannot keep food down. No new ability appears.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** Elias's off-book readings can damage chain of custody and the reliability of the very evidence he wants to protect.
**Ending type:** cost.
**Next-chapter pull:** The contaminated reading still points toward Niko Senn, who has been referred to a Kettle Rows clinic and is scheduled to be transferred before morning.

### Chapter 0007 — The Mechanic's Hands

**POV:** Nadia Kest.
**Location/time:** A transit maintenance shed and a damp service tunnel in The Cut, night.
**Immediate goal:** Find Jules before someone else does and determine whether he built the carrier frame or was framed by it.
**Resistance:** Jules has already moved Niko Senn out of the referral clinic's reach and will not say where, because he believes the police will hand him to the Concord. Nadia is also the officer who has to act on Elias's documented conflict of interest while finding a man who will not talk to her, which is not the work she is good at.
**Information revealed:** Jules knows a working frame needs a source anchor and a carrier, and he recognizes the release pattern as a design he was told was obsolete. He did not build the frame but copied its measurements, and he has never met the person commissioning them. He has moved Niko Senn into Sana Okafor's Kettle Rows clinic, the one address the rehabilitation account cannot reach, and he will not put that on the record before he is arrested.
**Action or decision:** Jules returns the remaining borrowed tools, admits that he performed unregistered repairs under a false clinic account, and asks Nadia to record the confession. Nadia brings him to Elias without treating him as an automatic suspect. Elias apologizes for using him as evidence first.
**Emotional change:** Nadia's trust in Elias depends on his accepting the correction; Jules's cooperation depends on Elias releasing the assumption of guilt.
**Power or resource change:** Elias gains a physical guide to the frame and a practical anchor for later work; he does not gain a magical upgrade. Nadia gains a suspect who will testify, which is a different kind of asset and one she has to keep alive.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** Jules begins as a suspect and becomes a reluctant technical ally through accountability, not romantic or magical shortcut. Niko Senn is the person Jules moved; he is not a separate patient.
**Ending type:** reversal.
**Next-chapter pull:** The frame's design was commissioned through a clinic account with no named patient, and the transfer order for Niko is issued in the morning.

### Chapter 0008 — The Clinic That Will Not Close

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** Kettle Rows Community Clinic, morning.
**Immediate goal:** Stop Niko Senn's unauthorized transfer and preserve the clinic's independence without turning it into a raid.
**Resistance:** Sana Okafor refuses to let Elias question patients under a Concord-style protocol; the clinic's patients need treatment more urgently than the police need a clean search, and the transfer order is legal on its face.
**Information revealed:** The clinic's treatment records have been merged with a rehabilitation account, and Niko is being moved under a certificate that names a treatment he does not remember receiving. The order's attached sequence is the same fabricated release found in Tamsin's threshold.
**Action or decision:** Elias asks Sana to choose which patients can be approached, stays outside the treatment rooms, and uses Nadia's legal authority to delay the transfer rather than forcing entry. He tells Niko what the delay costs him personally, including the file in his own building.
**Emotional change:** He loses control of the case in public but gains the clinic's cooperation, while Sana makes him confront the difference between protecting a person and possessing a story.
**Power or resource change:** Elias preserves a living anchor through consent instead of reading it; his First Margin remains costly and uncontrollable.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** Sana's clinic provides ordinary care, referrals, and witness networks. It is not a secret faction or a passive victim.
**Ending type:** reversal.
**Next-chapter pull:** The transfer order's sequence is the copied release, and the Proof Registry asks Elias to countersign a preliminary certificate before the hearing.

### Chapter 0009 — The Missing Release

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** Saint Orra Reach laboratory, late afternoon.
**Immediate goal:** Compare Niko's remembered order, the clamp, and the room residue without forcing a single account, and decide what he will do about the certificate he has been asked to sign.
**Resistance:** Niko's false rehearsal is convincing, Mara's instruments disagree with Elias's Margin, the Registry's draft certificate is due before sunset, and the Registry's file now notes that the assigned detective lives at the scene.
**Information revealed:** The copied working contains a fabricated release placed after an anchor, address, and turn. The release appears before the physical harm but is absent from the victim's own working, and the sequence repeats across the room and the clamp in a way that only Mara's measurements can call a template. The old phrase in the Morrow Street file means the same act, and Elias wants to compare the two records badly enough that he has to remind himself he is not permitted to pull the file. The draft certificate also says something different from what the public will be told: the public line is a contained accident involving an unregistered ward tool, while the certificate records a complete self-directed working by Tamsin, no third-party address, no contradicting witness.
**Action or decision:** Elias labels the disagreement rather than hiding it and refuses to countersign the certificate. He asks Mara to publish her own instrument readings from the threshold plate she lifted in Chapter 2, under her own name and without his — not the clamp, which he contaminated, and not his reading, which is not evidence. He dates the plate and signs the corner where anyone can see his name on it.
**Emotional change:** He admits publicly that his reading is incomplete and that his own residency makes him the easiest person in the room to distrust. The loss of certainty is painful, and Niko is not made to prove consent through suffering.
**Power or resource change:** Elias can recognise the same act recurring in three dead things — the threshold plate, the clamp, and the sequence attached to Niko's transfer order — and cannot yet interrupt it. He does not read Niko. Mara's instruments are what make it a template rather than a coincidence, and he trades certainty for a usable independent record.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** The central mystery begins with a copied release, not a hidden monster. A certificate's wording is a choice someone made, and Elias's refusal is the first time he loses standing by protecting a document's integrity.
**Ending type:** decision.
**Next-chapter pull:** Someone returns to the clinic at night for the carrier and its template before the dispensary's stock can be copied.

### Chapter 0010 — The Room Behind the Dispensary

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** Kettle Rows Community Clinic, the dispensary stockroom and back corridor, night.
**Immediate goal:** Stop the crew from carrying a second carrier and its template out of the stockroom, and keep the clinic's ward protection and its patients intact.
**Resistance:** Elias has no counterturn and no safe reading, and the working is too large to stop by refusing to look at it. The stockroom is narrow and full of glass, the carrier was built for the clinic's own ward circuit by someone who had access to the building, and Elias has a corridor and no arrest team. Nadia holds the far end of it because holding the far end is the agreement she extracted, and she is the reason he is not alone in a building that knows his address.
**Information revealed:** Elias can follow the causal order — anchor, address, turn, then the fabricated release — but still cannot see who gave the order. Jules identifies the carrier's physical route and the ward circuit it is feeding, and Mara measures the seam from outside the room instead of touching a person. The clinic frame is not Jules's clamp. Its tooth pattern is finer, its third stamp is cleaner than either of the first two, and it was cut from measurements rather than from a tool, so it is better made than the last one. Somebody is producing these in graded series, and this one was built to be used, not to be the first attempt.
**Action or decision:** Elias refuses to force a reading on Niko or any patient. He uses ordinary police procedure to seal the corridor, asks Jules to cut the carrier at its physical binding, and breaks the frame himself when the crew will not stop. The working stops when the carrier breaks. The operator escapes through the service door with the template's measurement, not the template.
**Emotional change:** He chooses a messy survival over a clean arrest, accepts that Mara's limits were part of the solution, and realizes the crew came prepared for someone who can read workings. Afterward he sits on the curb for forty minutes while three residents, one at a time, tell him what it felt like when the ward light went out. He is not the only one who has been studying him, and he is the only one who has to hear this part.
**Power or resource change:** The First Margin holds the order steady long enough for Jules to act on it, and no longer than that. Afterwards Elias cannot stand unaided, has a nosebleed, and loses forty minutes he can only account for from a transit log. He remains at First Margin and has no counterturn at all. Three residents lose ward protection for nine minutes while the circuit resets, and the dispensary stockroom is unusable.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** The clinic is standing, no patient is used as a live anchor, the operator escapes, and no dangerous-working certificate has been issued. The public case remains open; Elias has a broken frame, the seam and the graded stamps preserved on Mara's salt-glass plate, a refused signature, and a documented conflict of interest.
**Ending type:** earned hook.
**Next-chapter pull:** Someone has been inside the clinic's ward circuit with a frame and nobody checked the building's access log; Niko's transfer order is reissued under a rehabilitation certificate naming a treatment he never received; and Elias works out that the crew did not design this frame at all — they were handed a better-made version and told where to put it. The larger question is now who is training the network, not who carried this first frame.
