# Volume 01 Batch 0001 Outline — Chapters 1–10

**Batch range:** Chapters 1–10.
**Batch goal:** Establish Elias's ordinary work, the Veyra setting, the Sequence Sight rule, and the first concrete copywork case; give the team a small victory and a cost while making the copied fourth act impossible to ignore.

## Starting condition

Elias Venn is a Sequence Desk detective sergeant with an inactive legal file on the Morrow Street ward collapse, a damaged left ear, and no public authority for the Margin faculty. Nadia Kest is his police partner. Mara Quill is a skeptical forensic residue mage attached to Saint Orra Reach. A Kettle Rows night-bus dispatcher, Tamsin Ro, has been found dead in a rented room above a repair shop. Her door and personal working appear to show that she opened the room and harmed an arriving tenant.

## Starting relationships

- Elias and Nadia work well together but disagree about whether a raw reading can justify an emergency action.
- Elias and Mara have not yet agreed on consent, chain of custody, or what Elias's ability counts as evidence.
- Sana Okafor and the Kettle Rows clinic trust patients more than official certificates, but they do not yet trust Elias with a living trace.
- Jules Ardent is absent from the opening and enters as a mechanical suspect, not an ally.

## Starting power level

**First Margin:** Elias can perceive a fresh working's causal order after contact. He cannot read motive, identity, or a living person's inner trace; he cannot interrupt a working. The first reading will cost him time and physical stability.

## Batch midpoint

By Chapter 5, Elias and Mara have identified a source object and saved the intended target from immediate harm, but the witness's account contradicts the residue. Elias has a small victory and a serious doubt: a trained memory can make a sequence look false or true without lying.

## Batch climax

In Chapter 10, a copywork working begins in a crowded Kettle Rows clinic. Elias cannot safely perform a counterturn yet, so he uses ordinary police procedure, Jules's physical knowledge, and a last-minute warning to keep the intended victim from becoming the source anchor. The working stops, the attacker escapes, and the official response calls the case a contained accident.

## Required character changes

- Elias moves from confident private reading toward accepting that Mara's measurements and a witness's disagreement are necessary evidence.
- Nadia begins protecting Elias's access to cases while insisting that he stop working alone.
- Mara sets a consent boundary and treats Elias as a colleague with a dangerous limitation, not a useful magical shortcut.
- Jules changes from suspect to cautious technical ally after he returns the borrowed tools, records his unregistered repairs, and accepts responsibility for what he did.
- Sana's clinic becomes a moral and practical stakeholder rather than a location to be raided.

## Required power changes

- Elias gets one clear, controlled First Margin reading and one failed or overextended reading.
- The reading reveals a fabricated release standing in for one the victim never performed, but not identity or intent.
- The reading costs Elias dizziness, nausea, lost minutes, and a brief nosebleed.
- No new active spell is learned. The temptation to act is stronger than his current ability.

## Required world reveals

- Magic is embedded in modern work: a bus depot ward, a clinic residue lab, a door working, and a certified sequence record.
- A working follows a causal order, not a video replay.
- Copywork needs a source anchor and a carrier; the first network's crude frame can carry a stolen release template but cannot yet insert a correction independently.
- A witness's remembered order can be trained, so the Margin can be fooled.
- The official certificate records only the account that the proof weave can stabilize; the original release that the raw fourth act conceals remains outside it.

## Open questions after the batch

- Who supplied Orin Sable with the source object and the stolen release template, and who taught him to move a working through a living patient?
- Why does the copied sequence contain the same fabricated release as the Morrow Street report?
- Who trained the surviving witness, and what did they see before the rehearsal began?
- Why did the official certificate omit the witness who saw the order change?
- Can Mara's instruments measure the seam without touching a living patient?

## Chapter Cards

### Chapter 0001 — The Door That Opened Twice

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** Kettle Rows, Elias's repair-shop apartment and a rented room above it, just after midnight.
**Immediate goal:** Secure the scene before the Concord inspector arrives and determine whether Tamsin Ro's death is an ordinary trespass or a magical working.
**Resistance:** The room's door ward has failed in a way that makes the physical lock and the magical threshold disagree; Elias is also carrying an old, unlogged memory of a similar door in the Morrow Street file.
**Information revealed:** Tamsin used a small return-home door working; the room has two different residue orders in its threshold.
**Action or decision:** Elias stops the first responders from moving the door, records the room before touching the frame, and calls Nadia rather than relying on the old case memory.
**Emotional change:** The case feels familiar in a way that makes him distrust his own first reaction, but asking Nadia for help costs him the fantasy of controlling the scene.
**Power or resource change:** His first deliberate Sequence Sight contact leaves him dizzy and missing four minutes; he has one unstable reading and no certificate.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** Tamsin Ro is the first victim; her death is linked to a copied working, not a self-directed attack. The red thread in Elias's old file remains tied.
**Ending type:** discovery.
**Next-chapter pull:** The door's residue does not match the room's physical lock, and someone on the street is watching a police van arrive.

### Chapter 0002 — A Room With Two Orders

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** The same rented room, after the first responders establish a perimeter.
**Immediate goal:** Separate ordinary physical evidence from magical residue and keep the Concord inspector from sealing the room before Mara arrives.
**Resistance:** The victim's personal belongings suggest a fight, while the residue suggests a working that began after the door opened; the apparent witnesses give incompatible times.
**Information revealed:** The sequence contains a source anchor inside the room and a fabricated release that occurred after the working's turn but before the intended harm became physical. The victim's last working was copied rather than simply interrupted.
**Action or decision:** Elias photographs the physical scene, leaves the threshold untouched, and asks Mara to bring a salt-glass plate and an independent witness log.
**Emotional change:** He recognizes the missing release from a phrase in the Morrow Street file but refuses to let the old case dictate the new evidence.
**Power or resource change:** He chooses not to take a second reading while the room is unstable; this costs him a chance to solve the case quickly and protects the sample.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** The sequence is causal order. Elias can see an order but not the motive behind it.
**Ending type:** cost.
**Next-chapter pull:** Mara finds a thin seam in the residue that matches neither the victim nor the door.

### Chapter 0003 — The Margin

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** Temporary evidence room at the Sequence Desk, late morning.
**Immediate goal:** Determine whether the seam is an old repair, a copywork frame, or a false witness before the Proof Registry asks him to sign a standard certificate.
**Resistance:** The residue is too fresh for a reliable comparison, and Elias's left ear rings when he enters the room.
**Information revealed:** Elias's faculty is not a vision of people; it is a short perception of the order in which residue changed. The fabricated release is real and physically supported.
**Action or decision:** He accepts a limited, logged reading and records the exact moment it starts and ends. A single panel-like margin note appears: **MARGIN — A release follows the turn. A second trace begins before it.** The note makes him choose not to force a living-trace reading; he waits for Mara and the witness to set the next boundary.
**Emotional change:** The gift feels less like a gift and more like a responsibility he cannot delegate.
**Power or resource change:** The reading causes vertigo, a nosebleed, and a four-minute memory gap. Elias cannot touch a living person after it.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** The Margin gives order and omissions, never intent, identity, or legal proof.
**Ending type:** discovery.
**Next-chapter pull:** The only living trace that remains belongs to the intended target, who is refusing to speak to police.

### Chapter 0004 — The Witness Who Was There

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** Saint Orra Reach, a locked interview room with Mara and the intended target, **Niko Senn**.
**Immediate goal:** Obtain a safe account of what happened without asking Niko to submit to a second working or a coercive memory test.
**Resistance:** Niko remembers opening the door, but the order of the room's sounds is wrong; Mara insists that Elias stop asking leading questions.
**Information revealed:** Niko was made to rehearse a short sequence before the attack, possibly under pain or a disguised clinical instruction. A false rehearsal can put a different order in an inner trace.
**Action or decision:** Elias stops the interview, gives Niko the choice to speak without a recording, and lets Mara explain the consent boundary in plain language.
**Emotional change:** Niko's uncertainty frustrates Elias, but he recognizes that treating frustration as evidence of deception would be another form of control.
**Power or resource change:** Elias does not read Niko's living trace. He loses the fastest route to a resolution and gains a witness who will agree to independent care.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** False rehearsal alters remembered order but cannot create a physical injury by itself.
**Ending type:** relationship turn.
**Next-chapter pull:** Niko remembers a brass tool marked with Jules Ardent's repair number.

### Chapter 0005 — The Small Repair

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** Kettle Rows repair shops and a hardware archive, afternoon.
**Immediate goal:** Find the source object and stop the first copywork operator from moving it before the room's evidence disappears.
**Resistance:** The tool is ordinary, widely used, and missing from Jules's workshop; the operator has left a maintenance ticket that makes him look responsible.
**Information revealed:** Jules Ardent built a portable ward clamp, not a working frame. He admits lending the clamp to a clinic account under a false name for an unregistered repair; someone has since used its tooth pattern to carry Tamsin's copied release.
**Action or decision:** Elias follows the repair number through three shops, records each transfer, and brings the tool to Mara without taking it into police custody.
**Emotional change:** He feels the first clean satisfaction of the case, then realizes the satisfaction came from treating Jules as a source rather than a person.
**Power or resource change:** The physical clamp gives him a second connected anchor, but he has not gained a new power stage.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** Jules is a ward mechanic who can explain physical anchors but cannot read sequences.
**Ending type:** small victory.
**Next-chapter pull:** The clamp was delivered to a legal rehabilitation clinic that has no patient matching its intake records.

### Chapter 0006 — What Elias Touched

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** Sequence Desk evidence room, evening.
**Immediate goal:** Compare the clamp with the room residue before the Concord inspector can reclassify it as an ordinary maintenance object.
**Resistance:** The proof registry has issued a preservation order, and Elias believes the sample will disappear if he waits for a full hearing.
**Information revealed:** The clamp carries the same fabricated release as Tamsin's room, but the source object is not the clamp itself; the clamp is only a carrier.
**Action or decision:** Elias touches the clamp without logging the contact and gets a second, overwhelming reading. The sample becomes contaminated by a gesture that does not belong to the working.
**Emotional change:** He recognizes his own compulsion in a procedural failure. Mara refuses to use the contaminated reading, and the first useful evidence nearly disappears because of him.
**Power or resource change:** Elias gains a clearer sense of the sequence but loses physical stability, confidence, and unrestricted sample access. No new ability appears.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** Elias's off-book readings can damage chain of custody and the reliability of the very evidence he wants to protect.
**Ending type:** failure.
**Next-chapter pull:** The contaminated reading still points toward a patient at a Kettle Rows clinic who is scheduled to be transferred before morning.

### Chapter 0007 — The Mechanic's Hands

**POV:** Nadia Kest.
**Location/time:** A transit maintenance shed and a damp service tunnel in The Cut, night.
**Immediate goal:** Find Jules before someone else does and determine whether he built the carrier frame or was framed by it.
**Resistance:** Jules has already moved a patient to safety; he refuses to tell Nadia where because he believes the police will hand him to the Concord.
**Information revealed:** Jules knows a working frame needs a source anchor and a carrier, and he recognizes the release pattern as a design he was told was obsolete. He did not build the frame but copied its measurements.
**Action or decision:** Jules returns the remaining borrowed tools, admits that he performed unregistered repairs under a false clinic account, and asks Nadia to record the confession. Nadia brings him to Elias without treating him as an automatic suspect. Elias apologizes for using him as evidence first.
**Emotional change:** Nadia's trust in Elias depends on his accepting the correction; Jules's cooperation depends on Elias releasing the assumption of guilt.
**Power or resource change:** Elias gains a physical guide to the frame and a practical anchor for later work; he does not gain a magical upgrade.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** Jules begins as a suspect and becomes a reluctant technical ally through accountability, not romantic or magical shortcut.
**Ending type:** alliance.
**Next-chapter pull:** The frame's design was commissioned through a clinic account with no named patient.

### Chapter 0008 — The Clinic That Will Not Close

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** Kettle Rows Community Clinic, morning.
**Immediate goal:** Protect a patient from an unauthorized transfer and preserve the clinic's independence without turning it into a raid.
**Resistance:** Sana Okafor refuses to let Elias question patients under a Concord-style protocol; the clinic's patients need treatment more urgently than the police need a clean search.
**Information revealed:** The clinic's treatment records have been merged with a rehabilitation account, and a patient is being moved under a certificate that names a treatment no patient remembers receiving.
**Action or decision:** Elias asks Sana to choose which patients can be approached, stays outside the treatment rooms, and uses Nadia's legal authority to delay the transfer rather than forcing entry.
**Emotional change:** He loses control of the case in public but gains the clinic's cooperation, while Sana makes him confront the difference between protecting a person and possessing a story.
**Power or resource change:** Elias preserves a living anchor through consent instead of reading it; his First Margin remains costly and uncontrollable.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** Sana's clinic provides ordinary care, referrals, and witness networks. It is not a secret faction or a passive victim.
**Ending type:** moral reversal.
**Next-chapter pull:** The transfer order bears the same fabricated release as Tamsin's copied working, and a patient begins to remember the order incorrectly.

### Chapter 0009 — The Missing Release

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** Saint Orra Reach laboratory, late afternoon.
**Immediate goal:** Compare the patient's remembered order, the clamp, and the room residue without forcing a single account.
**Resistance:** The patient's false rehearsal is convincing, Mara's instruments disagree with Elias's Margin, and the official certificate is due at sunset.
**Information revealed:** The copied crimes all contain a fabricated release placed after an anchor, address, and turn. The release appears before the physical harm but is absent from the victim's original working. The same pattern appears in a partial old record connected to Morrow Street.
**Action or decision:** Elias labels the disagreement rather than hiding it, asks Mara to publish the physical measurement, and ties his old red thread around the plate without using it as a magical shortcut.
**Emotional change:** He admits publicly that his reading is incomplete. The loss of certainty is painful, but the patient is not forced to prove consent through suffering.
**Power or resource change:** Elias can identify the repeated fourth-act pattern but cannot yet interrupt it; he trades certainty for a usable independent record.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** The central mystery begins with a copied release, not a hidden monster.
**Ending type:** discovery.
**Next-chapter pull:** A second copywork working begins in the clinic while the Concord inspector arrives to seal the lab.

### Chapter 00010 — The Safe Version

**POV:** Elias Venn.
**Location/time:** Kettle Rows Community Clinic and the adjoining street, night.
**Immediate goal:** Stop the copywork attempt before the patient's living trace becomes a source anchor, while leaving the clinic and its residents intact.
**Resistance:** The working is too complex for Elias's current counterturn, the room is crowded, and the Concord inspector orders the police to preserve a single account.
**Information revealed:** Elias can see the causal order—anchor, address, turn, then the fabricated release, the fourth act—but still cannot see who gave the order. Jules identifies the carrier's physical route, and Mara gets the patient to choose a safe anchor.
**Action or decision:** Elias refuses to force a reading on the patient. He uses ordinary police procedure to clear the room, asks Jules to cut the carrier, and physically pulls the intended target from the frame. The copied working stops when the carrier is broken, not because Elias defeats a practitioner in combat.
**Emotional change:** He chooses a messy survival over a clean arrest, accepts that Mara's limits were part of the solution, and realizes the attacker has left a source object behind.
**Power or resource change:** The First Margin confirms the fourth-act pattern; Elias suffers vertigo, a nosebleed, and a fresh memory gap. He remains at First Margin and cannot yet perform a reliable counterturn.
**Continuity fact to preserve:** The clinic is safe for now, the operator escapes, and the official certificate calls the event a contained accident. The public case is not the complete truth.
**Ending type:** earned hook.
**Next-chapter pull:** At dawn, Mara finds that the source object is a memory clinic ledger, and Elias finds the same red-thread notation on the old Morrow Street file. The larger question is now who is training the network, not who made this first frame.
