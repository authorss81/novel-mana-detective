# Volume 01 Outline — The Missing Fourth Act

**Volume range:** Chapters 1–50.
**Central pressure:** linked magical homicides copy their victims' last workings while the Sequence Desk is pressured to certify a safe, simple account of each case. The first network can carry a stolen release template, but it cannot yet insert a correction independently; the missing technique is the next volume's question.

## Starting state

Elias Venn is a 36-year-old detective sergeant in the Veyra Civic Police Sequence Desk. He carries a twelve-year-old complaint about the Morrow Street ward collapse, an inactive legal file for a disaster that killed twenty-three people before the false attribution narrowed it to one maintenance worker. He still believes Teodor Rusk was made to carry responsibility for a correction he could not see, and treats a privately kept sequence reading as more reliable than an official certificate. He is competent, isolated, and physically affected by the hearing loss in his left ear.

The Quiet Grid is stable enough for ordinary city life but has begun intermittent failures. The Civic Concord wants dangerous cases closed quickly. The Sequence Desk has authority to certify, not power to compel every witness to submit to a living trace.

## Starting relationships

- **Elias and Nadia Kest:** long-term investigative partners; Nadia trusts his work and distrusts his habit of acting alone.
- **Elias and Mara Quill:** guarded professional friction; Mara's residue instruments and consent boundaries keep him from treating the Margin as proof by itself.
- **Elias and the Civic Concord:** adversarial dependence; the Police need certificates, and the Concord controls the standards those certificates follow.
- **Kettle Rows residents and the clinic network:** wary of official readings but not uniformly anti-magic; they help or obstruct according to immediate safety.

## Starting power level

**First Margin:** Elias can perceive the order of a fresh magical working after physical contact or entry into a residue scene. He cannot read motive, identity, intent, or the future; he cannot safely read a living person; he cannot interrupt a working. A reading causes dizziness, nausea, and lost minutes. He has no public name for the faculty and no reliable way to prove it in court.

## Major locations

- **Kettle Rows:** apartment rooms, the repair shop below Elias's home, community clinic, night market, and the first witness sites.
- **Saint Orra Reach:** Mara's independent residue laboratory and the hospital's emergency intake.
- **The Cut:** service tunnels, abandoned ward channels, and the physical places where copied frames are hidden.
- **Morrow Hill:** Sequence Desk offices, Concord Hall, and the public pressure of certificates and jurisdiction.
- **A private rehabilitation clinic:** a legal-looking site that begins as a lead and later proves connected to memory work.

## Major factions

- **Veyra Civic Police / Sequence Desk:** Elias's employer; capable but dependent on certified evidence.
- **Civic Concord:** Licensing, Ward Works, Clinical Practice, and Proof Registry staff who want dangerous cases closed and the grid protected.
- **Kettle Rows Community Clinic:** Sana Okafor and the mutual-aid network providing care outside the official system.
- **Orin Sable's copywork network:** unlicensed clinicians and frame-makers who steal working traces and hide a safe-looking sequence inside crimes.
- **Lantern Compact interests:** insurers and landlords who benefit from a narrow definition of magical harm.

## Escalation sequence

1. **The first copied victim:** a Kettle Rows resident dies in a room whose door and personal working appear to show a self-directed attack. Elias sees an order that does not match the room.
2. **The survivor and the frame:** a surviving witness remembers a different order. Mara identifies a physical seam, while Jules Ardent is suspected because his ward tools are missing.
3. **The clinic route:** the source anchor leads through a community clinic and a legal rehabilitation clinic. Sana's patients need protection before the Concord can secure the site.
4. **The certificate pressure:** the Proof Registry orders the Sequence Desk to certify a clean account before a public hearing. Elias's off-book reading is useful but cannot be admitted.
5. **The copied pattern:** separate victims begin carrying the same fabricated release. The pattern suggests a trained network rather than one frightened criminal.
6. **The old case echo:** the fourth act resembles the unknotted gap in the Morrow Street record, but Elias cannot yet connect it to Teodor Rusk without copying evidence.
7. **The frame workshop:** Jules and Mara help Elias identify a working frame in The Cut. The person who commissioned it is not Orin himself but a protected intermediary.
8. **The crowded-clinic working:** Orin attempts to complete a copied sequence in a clinic full of patients. Elias can stop it only by breaking the physical carrier with help; he cannot yet interrupt a working directly.
9. **The omitted witness:** the official certificate closes the immediate case but deletes the one witness whose order would prove the copywork. Nadia can keep the correction cell from escaping, but the city receives a reassuring version.
10. **The unresolved network:** the frame workshop is dismantled and several patients are safe, but a source anchor has already left Veyra. Elias's personal investigation now has evidence that someone above the clinic is teaching the method.

## Midpoint reversal

At the midpoint, the witness Elias trusted is not simply lying. They were trained through a false rehearsal while exposed to a real danger, so their inner trace carries a convincing but altered order. Elias's rare ability can be fooled when a living person is made to remember a different sequence. The case changes from “who copied the spell?” to “who gets to define the legal sequence?” and Elias's private certainty becomes a liability.

## Volume climax

At a crowded Kettle Rows clinic, Orin Sable activates a copywork working through a frame connected to a patient's personal object. Elias follows the causal order, identifies the fabricated release as the fourth act, and uses ordinary police procedure, Mara's warning, and Jules's physical anchor to break the carrier before the patient's living trace can be used as a permanent source. He stops the working, but the attack leaves Mara shaken, destroys part of the workshop, and allows Orin to escape with a source anchor.

Nadia secures the clinic and arrests two local operators. The official certificate records a complete self-directed working and omits the witness whose order would expose the copy. Elias is reprimanded for touching an unlogged sample, and the city announces that the dangerous case is closed. The public is safer; the record is cleaner than the truth.

## Concrete resolution

- Orin Sable's frame workshop in The Cut is dismantled and its stolen source objects are catalogued.
- The Kettle Rows clinic receives a temporary protection order, and the immediate victims receive care and a way to contest their official accounts.
- Two local copywork operators are charged; Orin is not caught, so the network remains active.
- Elias is reprimanded and loses unrestricted access to the first sample, but he keeps a private, documented copy of the physical discrepancy.
- Mara and Elias write an informal consent and contamination protocol. Jules is released as a suspect after he returns the borrowed tools and admits that he performed unregistered repairs under a false clinic account. He remains a reluctant technical ally.
- The Sequence Desk publicly treats the case as solved while the original release concealed by the fabricated fourth act, and the witness who would contradict it, remain outside the certified record.

## New question for Volume 02

Who is teaching Orin Sable to move a working through a living patient, and why does Aegis Mutual possess a certificate for a treatment no patient remembers receiving?

## Final chapter image

Elias stands outside the closed clinic with a salt-glass plate that contains two overlapping residue orders. The city has issued a single clean certificate. He wraps the plate in his red thread, but does not tie the knot; in the glass, the missing release continues to wait between the two accounts.
