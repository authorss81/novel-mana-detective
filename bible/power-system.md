# The Mana Detective — Power-System Bible

**Status:** Bootstrap canon. The system is designed to make investigation consequential, not to turn every scene into a stat screen. Elias's ability is a narrow sensory faculty; other practitioners have different training, resources, and costs.

## The physical rule

**Mana** is the ambient capacity for deliberate change. It gathers unevenly in water, metal, living tissue, and old ward-stone. A person does not spend a visible number of points. A working draws on a local current, changes a target, and leaves a residue while the current settles. Overworking a current causes **drift**: ward lights flicker, instruments disagree, records become unstable, and recent memories lose emotional force. Currents recover slowly, so repeated major workings leave a physical cost even when no one is injured.

A working is not the same as a spell label. It is a causal sequence of actions that changes something. The same result can be produced by different sequences, and a sequence can be incomplete.

## The core sequence

Every working has an **anchor**, an **address**, a **turn**, and a **release**, although a damaged or interrupted working may omit one or more of them.

1. **Anchor:** bind the working to an object, place, person, record, or social obligation. A living target is an especially strong anchor because it remembers contact.
2. **Address:** choose what the working acts upon and how the effect travels. A door can be addressed by its lock, its threshold, or the person who owns it; those choices are not interchangeable.
3. **Turn:** change the permitted state. A ward turns a fall into a stop; a memory clinic turns a remembered name into a believed one.
4. **Release:** commit the change and close the working. Releasing before the intended turn produces a live or backtracking working.

Three optional acts make advanced work possible:

- **Fold:** hide, redirect, or compress part of a working. Folds are why a spell can appear to happen in the wrong place.
- **Witness:** bind a living or recorded account to a residue so that the account can be certified. Witnessing does not make the account true.
- **Return:** send an unresolved consequence back along the working's connection. It travels to the nearest connected anchor that can contain it; if no anchor can contain it, the nearest living body—or the practitioner—absorbs the consequence.

The **sequence** is the causal order in which changes became possible and were committed. It is not a literal camera recording. Two sequences can contain the same gestures and produce different events if the anchor or release differs. The historical phrase **the fourth act** refers to a fabricated **release** inserted after an anchor, address, and turn, making a three-part working appear complete. It is not a fifth act and it does not prove that the release was the original cause.

## Residue and duration

**Residue** is the trace a working leaves in matter or life. It is not a visible mark and does not by itself identify a person. Duration depends on the material and the strength of the working:

- soft or living targets: minutes to hours for an ordinary working;
- metal, glass, stone, or a certified ward: days to months;
- a trained witness's **inner trace**: weeks, years, or indefinitely;
- a correction embedded in the proof weave: decades, with later edits layered over it.

An **inner trace** is the causal record held in a living nervous system after exposure. It is not a complete memory and does not contain intention. A person can remember a sequence in the wrong order because the mind learned the wrong rehearsal under stress. Physical residue can disagree with an inner trace; that disagreement is a clue, not a nuisance to discard.

## Elias's Margin

Elias has a rare, unregistered **Margin** faculty, formally called **Sequence Sight**. The Concord has no official list of people who can do it, while Elias's own readings can still be kept off the books. When he touches an object or enters the residue of a scene after a magical change, he perceives the order of the working that touched it. The perception is brief, sensory, and incomplete. It may feel like pressure, a change in balance, a remembered hand position, a sound before a sound, or a step that should be there and is not.

The Margin is a faculty, not a remote sensor. It does not work through ordinary photographs, phone audio, or a digital scan. It can read a secondary anchor only after Elias has physically located and identified it. It cannot read a person who has not been exposed to a working, and it cannot cross an unconnected material boundary.

### What the Margin gives him

- The order of acts that left residue.
- A missing or backward act when the omission is physically supported.
- A false start, fold, or interruption when the residue is fresh enough.
- Later, a seam where a working has been copied or corrected.

### What the Margin never gives him

- Identity, motive, consent, or the meaning of a spoken instruction.
- A replay of every person in the room.
- A date or location when the residue has no connection to either.
- A legal conclusion.
- Proof that a witness is lying. A witness can be honest and still carry a false rehearsal.

Early in the story, the Margin appears only as an involuntary perception. As Elias trains, the narration can occasionally use a rare, restrained panel. The panel is a short warning from the faculty, not a menu or a reward. At most one should appear in a chapter, and only when the rule changes the decision before Elias. A panel may look like this in the manuscript:

> **MARGIN — A release follows the turn. A second trace begins before it.**

The exact typography is flexible. The panel must not explain more than Elias can currently understand.

### Unconsented living contact

Before the Living Record stage, Elias cannot safely read a living person's inner trace. A brief order may leak through during an emergency, but contact can be interpreted by the living trace as an unconsented working. The target may lose a recent association, repeat a protective gesture, or suffer a phantom sensation; Elias takes the matching sensory and emotional bleed. This is not a smaller version of the later ability and is never a reliable investigative method. After Volume 1, any such choice must be written as a dangerous failure with consequences, not a convenient shortcut.

## Copywork and the fourth act

**Copywork** is an illegal method of replaying a prior working. A practitioner needs a source anchor, usually a victim's last personal item, a piece of the victim's body, or a trained witness, and a frame that can carry the order. Copywork can reproduce the visible and causal shape of a working, but it is expensive, emotionally dangerous, and usually leaves a **seam**: a place where the copied sequence has no live change of its own.

The seam is easy to miss in ordinary residue. It becomes visible to Elias only after he has learned to compare two versions of the same working and to accept that a reading can be incomplete. Mara's instruments and ordinary witnesses provide the corroboration that the Margin cannot.

**False rehearsal** is a memory-conditioning method used on a living target. A practitioner repeatedly rehearses an order while the target holds a physical anchor and experiences a controlled version of the associated danger. If the rehearsal is deep enough, the target's inner trace can carry the rehearsed order. The false rehearsal cannot make a door open by itself, move a body, or create a physical injury. It can make the person remember a different order, consent to a different target, or become unable to distinguish a real working from a prepared one.

The combination of copywork and false rehearsal makes a crime appear to be the victim's own spell. It is the distinctive weakness of Elias's power: a trained victim can give him a convincing sequence that is false. The correct response is not to distrust all witnesses. It is to look for physical seams, independent records, and the interests that benefit from one account.

## The proof weave and corrections

The **proof weave** is the Civic Concord's public record system. It combines a residue sample, a trained witness's inner trace, paper and digital records, and the Quiet Grid's stabilizing current. Its purpose is to make dangerous workings legible to courts and infrastructure. Its strongest output is a **certificate**: a statement that a sequence has been checked against a standard witness and anchor.

A **correction** is an authorized or stolen working that inserts a missing act into a record so that two incompatible sequences can be reconciled. It does not rewrite a person's entire past by wishing. It works through a target anchor, a witness network, and the grid. A correction can:

- make the official sequence show a cause after an effect;
- align several witnesses to the same order;
- redirect a consequence from a dangerous district to a less visible one;
- make a working legally appear completed or voluntarily performed;
- suppress a person or incident from benefits, employment, custody, or insurance.

It cannot reliably erase all raw physical evidence, and it cannot make a living person forget every event without leaving wider damage. When it affects memory, the result is drift: lost associations, broken trust, wrong names, and an inner trace that no longer matches ordinary recollection.

The first correction after the Blackline Event was a genuine emergency act. Later Quiet Hands users turn it into a way to shape blame, hide a crime, and keep a ward stable. The system's final danger is not that it is intelligent. It is that it rewards the person who makes everyone agree before anyone asks whether the agreement should exist.

## Counterturn and other active magic

A **counterturn** is a baseline technique available to trained practitioners, but its use requires an understood sequence, a physical anchor, and a practitioner willing to carry the Return. Elias's second stage is his first attempt; his sixth stage is his first reliable team-scaled interruption. A failed counterturn can leave him carrying the Return consequence: paralysis, a second injury, a false memory, or a working that begins inside his own body.

Other practitioners have bounded specialties: Ward Works can reinforce a route but cannot read intent; Clinical Practice can stabilize a body but can be coerced into treating an unlawful change as necessary; Proof Registry work can certify or withhold a certificate but cannot create a residue. No specialty is a universal answer. The plot must make people choose among imperfect options. The word **witness** therefore has two connected meanings: it is a magical act that binds testimony to residue, and it is the civic practice of keeping independent people able to contest that testimony.

## Power stages

These are craft milestones, not game levels. Each takes practice, failed experiments, a relationship decision, and a physical or social cost. The stage names are private shorthand in the bible; the prose should not display a level number.

### 1. First Margin — Volume 1

Elias can read a fresh, completed sequence after touching a scene. He sees the ordinary order and sometimes notices a final act that does not fit. He cannot read living people safely, cannot interrupt a working, and cannot tell copywork from a genuine sequence. A reading leaves him dizzy, nauseated, and missing the last few minutes of an ordinary evening.

### 2. Fracture — Volume 2

He learns to distinguish a false start, a fold, and an omitted act. He can attempt a simple counterturn if he has a physical anchor and a partner who can warn him. A wrong interruption makes his body repeat the next act or leaves him with a new phantom pain. The stage is earned by treating Mara's measurements and his own errors as data, not by training alone.

**EXECUTED, AND VOLUME 02 ENDS HERE. This is the ceiling: no Fragment stage, no Consensus Frame, no Living Record, no new power ceiling, and no stage name beyond Fracture may be used in Volume 03 without a new phase recording it first.**

**VOLUME 03 RECORDED BY THE PHASE THAT WROTE ITS OUTLINE AND ARC A, AS THAT RULE REQUIRES. The ceiling for Volume 03 is Fracture plus the BEGINNING of Threaded Evidence, and the beginning is not named in the prose.**

- **What the rule permitted and what it forbade.** Fracture is the standing ceiling. No Fragment stage exists. **Volume 03 may begin the first use of a connected secondary trace — reading a residue out of a record in contact with a working, and comparing it against another reading of the same working — and the comparison is more reliable than either reading alone, because a report and an event both leave their own residue and one of them was put there by a person with a pen.** No further ceiling is opened. No Consensus Frame, no Living Record, no counterturn performed on his own, no reading of a living person.
- **THE STAGE NAME IS NOT IN THE PROSE. "Threaded Evidence" is bible shorthand and appears in no chapter, in no dialogue, in no narration and in no document inside the world.** The prose for what he does in Volume 03 is *reading a record*, *reading it a second time*, *comparing two readings*, and *two independent numbers for the same four things*. A chapter that displays the stage name is wrong.
- **The gain, as delivered in Volume 03 Chapters 106 and 109.** He reads a hardbound night book in a refuge, in place, at about ten to ten and again at a quarter past eleven on one Wednesday night, having said out loud in the room that he cannot read a person and never will; and he reads a fire door at about twenty-five past eleven on a Tuesday, with a written consent, with four instruments and a third measurement beside him. **All three readings agree on four acts with the door last.** The volume's discovery is not a power and is not a stage: **a certificate can be built out of a true sequence by selection and reordering, and no instrument in the world can tell a selection from a fabrication, because a selection leaves exactly the same residue as a truth and a fabrication leaves more.**
- **The cost, as delivered, once.** **Emotional bleed.** A record in contact with a working carries what was in contact with it, including a person's feeling, and the feeling is not his and is not a vision of anybody's life. About four minutes. It goes off. He is hollow for a day and a half and is told nobody.
- **The two rules, which are the discipline of the stage and are binding on every later volume: he does not read the same object twice inside a month, and not at all if two readings disagree, and not on a night when he has been awake; and Mara Quill does not work on an object in contact with a person who is still in the building.** The second is a consent rule and outranks the first.
- **What it did not give him.** He still cannot read a person. He still cannot see motive, and the fact that a report and an event leave the same residue is exactly why his faculty cannot help him here. **He gained a way of being shown that the certificate is true and is wrong at the same time, and that is worth more than the reading was and it is not a victory.**

- **The gain, as delivered.** He can name a false start out loud in a room and be checked; in Arc C he was wrong about one in public and it cost a woman of sixty-one four nights of a drug, which is the stage's own cost being spent on the diagnosis rather than the act. **He can attempt a counterturn, and he can perform one, but only with an understood sequence, a physical anchor he made himself, and other people holding the room.** The first attempt, alone, on a door, in a cold corridor on 11 January 2030, **failed**: he put a blank in, and a blank is the one thing in a working that nothing can decide about. The first one that works is at 22 January 2030, at the volume's climax, on a person, in a room, at night, in public, **with a refusal in it and not committed**, and the release came to the place and could not close over it.
- **A counterturn is a sentence with a verb in it.** What he puts in has to be an act, and it has to be held by somebody who means it, and **if he does not mean it he is carrying somebody else's sentence.** A blank can be carried by anybody, which is why it is the thing a person is made to carry for a very long time. This is the whole of the difference between the failed attempt and the one that works, and it is not a technique difference.
- **THE FOUR PEOPLE WHO HELD THE ROOM, as Chapter 99 enumerates them, and the rule this establishes: no part of a counterturn may be substituted by his faculty.** A doctor of forty-seven who can keep eleven people flat. A police inspector of twenty-two years who is the reason eleven people can be in a room at all. A man of twenty-five in the shop below who was not asked and did not know. A man of thirty-nine at the other end of a corridor four miles away who can say *not yet* and then say *now*. **He is not in charge of any of them, chose none of them, and was not allowed to choose, and that is the stage's social content, not a detail of it.**
- **The measure of a hold is a room and not a clock.** He can hold an uncommitted act for about as long as four people can hold a room. **There is no number for it anywhere in the manuscript and none may be supplied**; the only figure in the record is the four minutes he announced in the room before it began, and it was not the length of it. Nobody knows how many times it will work.
- **THE NEW STANDING COST OF THE SUCCESS, AND IT IS NOT CURED: he carries a room.** From that night he cannot be in a room with eleven chairs in it without counting them, **and the count is always eleven**, and nobody can explain it. It is not pain, not a symptom, not a memory, not the order of a day, place or duration, and **it is not the repetition.** A room with nine chairs in it is nine both times and he is aware of the number the whole time he is in the room.
- **The wrong-interruption cost is spent and is not spent again: a body that repeats the next act.** It is uncured, the mechanism is unknown, and the team works around it in public rather than curing it.
- **What Fracture did not give him.** He still cannot read a living person, and he says so out loud in rooms, and it has stopped a constable's hand. He cannot interrupt a working by himself. He cannot separate a fold from a genuine turn, and a panel can contradict him about a drawing. He cannot tell a working is happening in a room by looking at it. **A first successful use of a stage is not a promotion, and Volume 03 does not begin with a bigger version of it.**

### 3. Threaded Evidence — Volumes 3–4

He can read a secondary trace from a connected object or record and compare two readings. The comparison is more reliable than either reading alone, but a digital copy records testimony, not the sequence itself. He must track every touch and cannot contaminate the sample. A living victim's inner trace can cause emotional bleed: grief, rage, or fear that belongs to another person.

### 4. Consensus Frame — Volume 5

With at least two independent witnesses and an independent physical measurement, he can reconstruct a sequence across a gap without pretending that disagreement has disappeared. A frame can support a decision, never replace consent. If Elias forces a frame without consent, his act can create a false residue and make ordinary witnesses appear to confirm a lie.

### 5. Living Record — Volume 6

With explicit consent from a living subject, he can read layered residue in a person or place and distinguish old workings from a recent one. He still cannot see motive or recover a deleted identity. The cost is a fragment of his own memory or a permanent sensitivity to the place; reading a home is not a harmless tour.

### 6. Counterturn — Volumes 7–8

He can interrupt a complex working if he understands its anchor, address, and release. The counterturn must be physically performed or coordinated with a team. He temporarily takes the unresolved Return, which can injure him or transfer part of the magical problem to a consenting anchor. He is not immune to the people he is trying to stop.

### 7. City Thread — Volumes 9–10

He can perceive how a record, ward, or infrastructure system routes a working through other anchors. He can locate the shape of a correction, not its author or purpose. City-scale exposure causes overload, temporary blindness, panic, and makes him legible to the proof weave. He needs a physical route, a trusted partner, and a reason to keep looking.

### 8. Open Record — Volumes 11–12

He can keep multiple contradictory sequences visible long enough for separate people to compare them. It requires a distributed witness network and shared consent; alone, he can only hold one frame and it will collapse into a new false order. The cost is a shared sensory burden on his allies and a growing inability to trust any single account, including his own.

### 9. Unclosed Window — Volume 13

In the final working, Elias can leave a consequential act unperformed long enough for other people to act. This does not rewrite history or freeze time. It holds a causal gap in the central proof weave, and it only works because other people maintain the surrounding anchors. The price is permanent loss of the Margin and a fragment of the memory of Hollis. He remains a competent detective, but he no longer sees a sequence before anyone else.

## Growth and failure rules

Every new capability must pass all four questions:

1. What physical or social practice made it possible?
2. What did it cost Elias or an ally?
3. What can an opponent exploit?
4. What choice becomes harder after he gains it?

Training scenes should change decisions. They should not be menus, repetitive exercises, or a reward for surviving a dangerous experiment. A stage can be used incorrectly and fail. A successful reading can still produce a false conclusion when the evidence is incomplete. A refusal to read can be a competent choice.

## Hard limits

- Elias cannot read intent, identity, or the future.
- He cannot make a person tell the truth, restore a deleted memory, or remove a working without consent and consequence.
- He cannot scan a city remotely or access a database as magical vision.
- He cannot summon an unlimited power source; every active working affects a current and a body.
- He cannot use a counterturn without an anchor and a precise understanding of the sequence.
- He cannot force a witness to consent through the Margin.
- He cannot solve every problem alone. A power gain that removes the need for relationships violates the series engine.
