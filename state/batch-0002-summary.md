## What the arc did

Arc A spent six of its ten chapters on investigation. This one spends them on **recovery, institutional cost, a stakeholder's price, a hearing, two scenes, a supply chain, a career's public refusal, a supplier's name, and a prohibition.** The batch goal — *find who supplies the frames and how the templates travel* — is met in Chapter 18, and the arc's second half is the **method**, not a man.

**The mechanism, which is the arc's real discovery:** the network does not suppress ward failures. It **answers** them. A fault is reported to the municipal ward line; a contractor on an approved list arrives the same day, is helpful, tells the resident the equipment is fine and hands them a card for the next man; the fault is closed as *resolved* on the day it was reported. The network therefore knows where the Quiet Grid is already blinking, the district's **reported faults** fall, the ward continuity premium falls with them, the district becomes **cheaper to insure**, and the ward maintenance contract is re-tendered at forty per cent less — so the wards get worse, so the cover deepens. That loop is the reason real failures are the perfect place to put a crime nobody investigates twice, and it is the batch's answer to *an insurance finding cuts a district's protection on a conclusion he can see coming and cannot prove*.
