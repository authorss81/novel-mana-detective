## The mechanism, in one sentence and one object

**Care has become a market, and the market's paper is a public three-credit tariff.** A woman of fifty-eight does a published procedure for eleven credits in a first-floor room above a dry cleaner, out of one side of A4 that cites a page of the Witness Training Compendium and carries **a third box the manual does not contain, and the third box is a release.** The practice is an offence in two particulars and no others: **it is unregistered, and it is undercharged.** That is why nobody prosecutes it and why nobody is frightened by it, and it is the shape of the whole volume.

**The restriction that matters is not criminal law. It is tariff note 4:** *no claim may be made under Schedule D in respect of a procedure at number 2 to 14; a charge for such a procedure is not a claim, is not to be settled by a third party, and may not be receipted by a person other than the practitioner performing it.* **Note 4 is two years old. The 1927 printing has three notes and the 1929 has four; the 1927 Schedule D stops at procedure 9; procedure 12 is not in the 1927 book at all; the revision is genuine and the date on it is in ink and not a printer's mark, because a printer marks a revision before he breaks the page.** Somebody at a meeting was asked to be accurate and was not, and the practice got older and then got tighter, and the woman at the bottom of it was never told either fact.

## The two questions, and what Arc A did to them

**Both were asked once, out loud, on Marshall Street, on 3 November 1929, and were answered by nobody. Neither is answered.**

- **Who is teaching a fitter with a card to move a working through a living patient.** **Answered in form and not in substance: nobody is teaching anybody, because there is no teacher.** What there is, in four documents, is a product — a four-line service card with a licence class in capitals at the foot, a printed sheet of A4, a counterfoil, an envelope of cash, and a published price list somebody is not charging. **There is no man in a good coat going round Kettle Rows fitting things to people's walking sticks; there was one man, one visit, one stick, on 24 October, and he was charged on 2 November at 09:35.** The technique is bought, and a market is a better delivery system than a network, and nobody has printed anything.
- **Why does Aegis Mutual hold a certificate for a treatment no patient remembers receiving.** **Not answered, and Aegis Mutual is not named once in Chapters 51–60, deliberately.** What is on the page instead is **three counterfoils with claim numbers, Schedule D service codes, and a margin that says 22 Cutlass Row 3rd fl and nothing else**, dated 4 May 1927, 19 January 1928 and 11 September 1929; **a tariff note that forbids the claim**; **a premises that has not been let since before the war**; a **temporary protection order with an end date on it**; **four ordinary people in a queue at a claims counter, each of whom is the case in a different shape**; and one sentence from a seventy-year-old man in that queue: *they have got the treatment and they have not got me.* **Every document in the arrangement is genuine and no claim in existence supports any of them, which is the reason the question cannot be asked the way it was asked on a pavement.**
