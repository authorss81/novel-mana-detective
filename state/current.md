## The discovery, and the rule that follows from it, and it is the series' spine

**A dangerous-working certificate in this case is not a lie. It is a selection.** Take a real four-act working, certify three of the four as a self-directed working performed by one man on his own person, and **leave the fourth out**. The act left out is the one that would have shown a second person in that room. It cost two credits and a signature and about four minutes, and there is no working in it and no magic.

**And there is no instrument that can ever show it, and this is the discovery the whole series has been walking towards and the reason the ending works:** a selection leaves exactly the same residue as a truth and a fabrication leaves more, so **the only way anybody will ever find out which one is which is a person.** The Margin cannot see the difference because a report and an event both happen in a page.
