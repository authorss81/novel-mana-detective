## Canon established

- **Series:** *The Mana Detective*, 650 chapters, thirteen volumes of about fifty. Urban fantasy mystery / procedural. One slow-burn romance only: Elias Venn and Mara Quill. No harem.
- **Protagonist:** Detective Sergeant Elias Venn, 36 at the opening, Sequence Desk, Veyra Civic Police. A narrow apartment above his repair shop in Kettle Rows. Hearing loss in his left ear. The faculty is **Sequence Sight**, which he experiences as the **Margin**.
- **First Margin (Volume 1 only):** perceives the causal order of a fresh working after contact or entry. Never motive, identity, intent, or the future. Cannot safely read a living trace. Cannot interrupt a working. Cannot tell copywork from a genuine sequence on his own — he can see a release standing where nobody performed one and can notice the same act recurring, but the diagnosis of *copy* and the seam belong to Mara's instruments. Costs dizziness, nausea, lost minutes, sometimes a nosebleed; vary what each reading takes from him.
- **Sequence order:** anchor → address → turn → release. The historical **fourth act** is a fabricated release *inserted* after those three by the first post-Blackline correction. The victim's original release is the act that goes missing. Do not call a normal malfunction a fourth act.
- **The unfinished act:** the first correction was a real emergency act, and its **central release was left incomplete**. The final antagonist is that established pattern — the **First Correction**, the citywide working assembled from the proof weave, Quiet Grid, living anchors, and old records, with Iona Saye as its human steward. No new final enemy may be introduced.
- **Ages that must reconcile:** Hollis Ward is late fifties at the opening, twenty at the Blackline Event 38 years ago. Teodor Rusk is dead before the opening; the Morrow Street collapse killed twenty-three. Iven Quill has been missing six years.
- **Institutions:** Veyra Civic Police / Sequence Desk; the Civic Concord (Licensing, Ward Works, Clinical Practice, Proof Registry); Proof Tribunal; Veyra Civic Council; the Quiet Grid; the Veyran Vault; Lantern Compact; Aegis Mutual; the Ninefold Assembly; the Quiet Hands. The Civic slogan is "No consequence without a witness."
