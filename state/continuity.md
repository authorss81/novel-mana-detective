## Canon established

- **Series:** *The Mana Detective*, 650 chapters, thirteen volumes of about fifty. Urban fantasy mystery / procedural. One slow-burn romance only: Elias Venn and Mara Quill. No harem.
- **Protagonist:** Detective Sergeant Elias Venn, 36 at the opening, Sequence Desk, Veyra Civic Police. A narrow apartment above his repair shop in Kettle Rows. Hearing loss in his left ear, permanently, and a tinnitus that began after a stairwell reading in Chapter 1. The faculty is **Sequence Sight**, which he experiences as the **Margin**.
- **First Margin (Volume 1 only):** perceives the causal order of a fresh working after contact or entry. Never motive, identity, intent, or the future. Cannot safely read a living trace. Cannot interrupt a working. **Cannot tell an assembled act from a performed one, and in Chapter 9 he is shown being wrong about that in front of the only person who can measure it** — he read the delivered release as a performed one. The diagnosis of *copy* and the seam belong to Mara's instruments. Costs dizziness, nausea, lost minutes, sometimes a nosebleed; vary what each reading takes from him. **In Volume 01 the nosebleed is Chapter 10's cost and no other chapter's, and no two readings may take the same thing.**
- **Sequence order:** anchor → address → turn → release. The historical **fourth act** is a fabricated release *inserted* after those three by the first post-Blackline correction. The victim's original release is the act that goes missing. Do not call a normal malfunction a fourth act.
- **The unfinished act:** the first correction was a real emergency act, and its **central release was left incomplete**. The final antagonist is that established pattern — the **First Correction**, the citywide working assembled from the proof weave, Quiet Grid, living anchors, and old records, with Iona Saye as its human steward. No new final enemy may be introduced.
- **Ages that must reconcile:** Hollis Ward is late fifties at the opening, twenty at the Blackline Event 38 years ago. Teodor Rusk is dead before the opening; the Morrow Street collapse killed twenty-three. Iven Quill has been missing six years. Hollis has been gone eleven years, and in Chapter 3 Elias describes him as a ward assistant who told him in his twenties that the faculty was a sensitivity and not a destiny.
- **Institutions:** Veyra Civic Police / Sequence Desk; the Civic Concord (Licensing, Ward Works, Clinical Practice, Proof Registry); Proof Tribunal; Veyra Civic Council; the Quiet Grid; the Veyran Vault; Lantern Compact; Aegis Mutual; the Ninefold Assembly; the Quiet Hands. The Civic slogan is "No consequence without a witness."

## World facts established in the manuscript during batch 0001

- **Rehabilitation account 44-9.** An account that is a legal person, with an address on Verge Row, a card, and a return clause on any tool issued against it. It cannot book a patient into the Kettle Rows Community Clinic address, so it reaches people by **merging files**: the patient's record is joined to the account's record and the account's own address becomes the patient's address of record. This is how a transfer order became legal on its face without anybody at the clinic having agreed to anything.
- **A salt-glass plate is not admissible unless it is on a Concord register**, and Mara Quill holds no Concord licence and has decided not to. Her material is the testimony of an uncertified forensic mage. This is her standing limitation and it must not be quietly solved.
- **A working can be delivered.** A release-shaped residue with no settling curve was made elsewhere, carried on a solid object, and seated into a slot that was already the right shape. What it arrived on — tool, card, coin, token, bead — is not knowable at First Margin. **Seam rule:** the same joint failing the same way in two different objects is a specification, not an accident.
- **A transfer of place of residence can carry a sequence box** with four acts in it, and a certified sequence can be attached to a document that is supposed to be housekeeping. A certificate is a document with chosen words; the Registry's public line and its draft certificate say different things, and both are already written.
- **Registry timing as a weapon:** a document request for a hearing pack tells whoever can read it exactly when records will be copied. This is how the batch ended.
