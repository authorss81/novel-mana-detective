# Character State

**Updated after Volume 01, batch 0001 (Chapters 1–10).** For the writer's handoff only. The authoritative descriptions live in `bible/characters.md`; this file records where each person physically and emotionally is at the end of Chapter 10, and what they now know that they did not know at the start.

## Elias Venn

- **Where:** Kettle Rows. His apartment over the shop, and increasingly at the Kettle Rows Community Clinic on Marshall Street, which he has no authority over and keeps turning up at.
- **Body:** left-ear hearing loss, permanent, and worse than it was on Monday. The ear went quiet in the stairwell at 01:52 and came back inside an hour; from Monday afternoon it did not come back, and the whole left side of his head is now gone rather than muffled, and he cannot locate a sound in a room without watching the mouth that made it. **No tinnitus — that is reserved for Volume 10 and the batch must not spend it.** Four readings spent, four different costs: four minutes and his balance; his hearing and three hours of the afternoon; two hours of shaking and nothing staying down; and in Chapter 10 both legs from the hip, forty minutes he can only account for from a transit log, and the volume's only nosebleed. He has not eaten properly since Monday. He makes strong coffee and does not drink it. He has started washing his hands and logging his own name, time and purpose before he touches anything, which is new and which he is not sure he likes.
- **Knows:** the working bound to a desk and addressed a person, and its release was not Tamsin Ro's; the release arrived already settled, so it was delivered; the same failure in the same joint appears in two objects, so the pattern is wrong twice; the fourth act is a box on a Concord form; the maker's pair is graded and there is a third cut better than his own; the account reaches people by merging files; Niko Senn was made to rehearse a door.
- **Wrong about:** that a release-shaped act in a threshold means a release was *performed* in that room. Mara Quill has corrected him with an instrument and he has accepted the correction in writing.
- **Gave up:** a second reading in Chapter 2 to protect a sample; the fastest route to Niko's account in Chapter 4; the clamp in Chapter 6; a countersignature in Chapter 9; control of the case in public in Chapter 8. He has never given up the old file, and he has now twice chosen not to open it.
- **Wants:** to get Niko Senn through the review board and past Monday. To keep his thumb off the foil stencil. To find the person who sent Jules a sealed envelope.
- **Cannot do:** interrupt a working, read a living trace, touch a sample, or offer a raw reading to a court.
- **The lie, updated:** he no longer believes one private certainty will save anybody. He has begun to believe that a labelled disagreement is stronger than an unlogged certainty, which is the volume's real gain and is not yet the same as believing it.

## Mara Quill

- **Where:** Saint Orra Reach residue laboratory, two rooms behind the hospital's linen office. Four instruments, two of them older than the department beside them.
- **Body:** no injury. She has not slept properly since Wednesday.
- **Holds:** two salt-glass plates in her own name, one sleeve signed by Elias beside her initial, one lifted on a pavement with Nadia Kest as witness. Eleven figures from a clamp she can no longer measure because he put a hand in it, and an instrument log in a building she has no key to.
- **Decided:** to put her four figures from the threshold plate into the file under her own name and without his, with him on the sleeve as a person who was in the room and nothing more. She has **asked for** the note that says she was looking at a number when his hand went into the channel to go into the file in those words; it is not written yet, and she has not softened it for him.
- **Will not:** let him touch a living trace, let him touch the plate, soften the record, or certify anything with a Concord register she does not hold. She has no Concord licence and has decided not to take one.
- **Pressure:** her standing is the ceiling on the whole case. A plate that is not on a register is the testimony of an uncertified mage, and in a year a Registry officer will be able to say that no instrument in Veyra found anything unusual in that room unless somebody writes it down. Somebody now has.
- **Wants:** independent material, written and living records that a patient did not have to surrender a body to produce. Her brother is six years missing and she has not said his name out loud yet.

## Nadia Kest

- **Where:** Serious Crimes Unit, and a car on Marshall Street.
- **Knows:** everything in the file, including that Elias's four readings are logged with their limits, and that he gave her the order to write them down.
- **Has done:** entered his residency declaration in the file; retained him on the case on the condition that he does not work alone, made in front of a witness; held a telephone warrant at 23:41 on Friday and undertaken to explain it in person to a magistrate at eight in the morning; taken a full voluntary statement from a mechanic so that he is a witness and not a defendant; and signed a broken frame over to a private laboratory as a transfer rather than a seizure.
- **Holding back:** a routing slip from Deputy Chief A. Orr asking for a summary of the Morrow Street sequence position by close of Friday. She has not told Elias. Friday has now passed and she still has not, which is the first time she has missed a thing she set herself.
- **Believes:** institutions can be saved by the right chain of command, even when the command is compromised, and she has just spent a telephone call proving she will bend that command before she will leave a working alone in a room with a frightened man in a bed.
- **Wants:** Elias kept and usable, and the department not to sacrifice its credibility to protect itself.

## Dr. Sana Okafor

- **Where:** the Kettle Rows Community Clinic, a former paint works on Marshall Street. Nineteen years awake at five in the morning. Forty-seven, and twenty-two of those years a doctor — use one number for both.
- **Has done:** kept Niko Senn under a clinical hold; filed a hold on a Witness, Operations account transfer; put a copy of the transfer order on a board in her waiting room where patients can read it; told Elias to his face that a case he keeps is a boy who does not exist outside a building; let nine people out of a stockroom corridor in the dark in nine minutes and did not make it a police matter; and let Ahmed photograph her own maintenance book in place rather than surrender it to anybody; and stood with her hands folded while a stranger signed it and told nobody, which is the cost she has actually paid so far and which nobody has yet had the decency to ask her about.
- **Believes:** the only thing that stops an institution taking a person is other people knowing what it wrote down. Care must be independent of a proof certificate.
- **Cost:** a stockroom she cannot use, a nine-minute hole in her wards, a man on her bins going over her wall, and a registrar with a clipboard in her corridor at eleven on a Friday morning.
- **Not yet:** a witness. Not a source, and she has told him so in terms.

## Jules Ardent

- **Where:** nine Pell Street, above a dry cleaner, door unlocked. Nadia's Chapter 7 instruction was Saint Orra at eight with a solicitor of his own choosing and nothing near Kettle Rows until it was resolved; Chapter 7 and Chapter 9 both have him at Saint Orra with that solicitor, and Chapter 10 has him in the clinic at 21:30 under her **written variation**, with her name under the reason.
- **Age/role:** 29, ward mechanic. Transit by night, small repair work by day, a bench with a wrong cut kept on it because throwing it away would be a lie.
- **Has done:** built six clamps, stamped JA-4412 to JA-4417; lent 4417 on the seventeenth of September to a man with a rehabilitation card for eighty credits and no paperwork; cut two sets from a sealed envelope containing a design for a device that holds a sequence in a channel, marked *deprecated practice, retained for existing frames only*; moved Niko Senn into the one address the account cannot reach; returned a canvas roll of tools; asked Nadia to record six years of unregistered repairs under a false clinic account, and been recorded; **driven the clamp back to Verge Row at 23:30 on Wednesday night to keep a promise he wrote in his own bench book**; and re-secured a conduit in a dispensary ceiling for nothing, refusing to let anybody pay him or put his name on it.
- **Holds:** the uncollected second set, in his bench room, from a commission he has never been paid the rest of.
- **Not cleared of anything.** Nobody has been charged. He believes the police will hand him to the Concord and a notice will go on a door, and he is not entirely wrong.
- **Want:** to keep repairing the grid without giving anybody else a private hold on it. **Fear:** that every practical compromise makes ordinary people more disposable.

## Niko Senn

- **Where:** the Kettle Rows Community Clinic, room two, on a fever of thirty-seven eight and a rash of undetermined origin.
- **Age/role:** 24, overnight attendant at a coin laundry on Verge Row, four nights, rides the last inbound and the first outbound. Has not slept since Wednesday, and on Friday he put the outbox number nineteen sixty-five back to a registrar who had not said it, before she had turned the sheet round, because a woman on the Registry's telephone line on Sunday night gave it to him as a thing to hold on to. Nobody in the case has established where he got it from yet.
- **Knows:** that his account of the room is in the wrong order and that it is not because he is lying. That he was made to rehearse an opening a door, four lines, three or four weeks ago, for forty credits, with a man's fingers on the back of his neck. That there is now a name for a treatment he never had and eleven days he never spent. That there is a piece of paper with his name on it and it is legal.
- **Refused:** the Registry's witness-binding, in his own words, unprompted by a question from the police desk.
- **Gave:** the maker's number JA-4417 and the description of the man in the smock, unrecorded, on his own terms. He also said, to Elias's face, that the police will use him.
- **Do not:** turn him into a witness to the room. He is a witness to the attempt, and that distinction is the reason the draft certificate does not have to name him.
- **Next:** a review board later in the week, a transfer order reissued for 06:00 Monday, a hearing on Monday the 24th, and an address the account cannot book into.

## Tamsin Ro

- Dead, and the first victim. Night-bus dispatcher, Orra depot, four years in the room, four years of clean attendance, a door ward she paid ninety credits and eleven minutes to seat, a kettle on the sill because the window leaks, a cat fed on Thursdays by Otto Vahl, who now has that cat in the back of a repair shop with a chair in it. She is publicly named as the first victim and the city has an information sheet that calls it a contained accident involving an unregistered ward tool.

- **Bess Yonkin**, tool registry clerk, nineteen years. Gave him the ledger page and refused the dies.
- **The stockroom operator.** Unnamed and to stay unnamed until Arc B. Tall-ish, not tall, very neat, a grey work jacket with the sleeves pushed up, a canvas holdall, a vernier calliper, a phone. **No smock and no canvas roll: he is the fourth anonymous figure and is not a composite.** He stayed on the ladder and kept turning a screw while Elias put copper under the clip, and he twice told him, without any hurry at all, that he would want to think about it. He went out through a gap in the door and across the yard, over the wall, and then stopped and photographed Elias on the paint bins before he rounded the corner. Calm. Left with a caliper and a phone and nothing else, which is why the frame and its stencil are still in the world. **He told a policeman a thing that was true in order to buy four seconds. Remember that when he is finally named.**
- **The maintenance man, Wednesday night.** Tall-ish, not tall, very neat, a Ward Works vest number, his own signature in a hand that does not belong to anybody in the building, and the job written beside it as *check the line*, with no counter-signature. He brought a transfer order in with him and left it on a bench, which is the only reason this case has an access trail. Nobody has ever seen his face, because nobody ever wrote one down.
