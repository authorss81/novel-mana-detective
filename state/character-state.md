- **Owes:** Niko Senn the whole of the 21 September intake, told to him on the Wednesday in a shop, unasked, because Niko had been told his withdrawal was used. Jules Ardent the corridor promise of 23 October and the promise not to ask for the card a sixth time. Marguerite Osei a reply he will not send, because an answer is correspondence and correspondence has a file on it.
- **Wants:** a sheet of A4 with five lines on it in an office, and about four years to be alive when the supplement's cross-reference is read by somebody who is frightened of nothing.
- **Cannot do:** interrupt a working, read a living person, tell a fold from a genuine turn, separate a delivered act from a performed one, and now, since Wednesday, tell how long anything has been going on. **First Margin, unchanged. The volume ends at the stage it began with.**
