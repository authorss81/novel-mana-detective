## What the arc did

The batch turns one question: *how can a witness's order be wrong without anybody lying?* The answer, delivered at the midpoint, is that a rehearsal can be run **through a live exposure**, and that the rehearsal is then true in every element and wrong only in the order — and that the order is the only thing anybody certifies.

**The three things the batch owns, in the order Chapter 25 puts them:**

1. **The recantation.** Niko Senn fills in **Form W-1 (rev. 3)** in an interview room, in his own hand, on the advice of the Concord's own leaflet, and does not lie about it. Field six is a stated reason and the reason is true.
2. **The truth underneath it.** He attended **Witness Services** on **Monday 24 September and Saturday 29 September**, and was walked through the **actual fourth-floor landing of 14 Kettle Rows** while the thing he was the address of was still live in him. The first four sessions gave him an invented room; the Concord's method is the same method with the same words; the real room, the real click and the real name laid themselves into the rehearsed slots, and the rehearsed order — **room, click, name** — won. **It is word for word the order Elias read off his own jamb at 01:52 on 17 September, and gave to the Registry by telephone eight days later.** The residue says the working began after the door opened. A man cannot be read. That is the ceiling, and the reversal uses it. **Three orders are now in play and must never be confused: (a)** the account he withdrew and the board minuted — **mug, click, room** — which does not fit the room; **(b)** the trained order he now holds — **room, click, name**; **(c)** Elias's own perception, which is the same as (b). **Schedule 1 records three reading occasions and four limitations and records no order at all.**
3. **The liability.** At tab 3 of the bundle produced in answer to the withdrawal is a **Witness, Operations intake sheet of 11 September**, and in a ruled box headed *description of order as perceived, by the assigned detective, to be used in witness support* is **Elias's own words**, entered at **14:22 on Friday 21 September** out of a telephone call in which he was asked a reasonable question by a competent official, and he read *to be used in witness support* as *supporting the witness-support file*. The office that accepted it is **J. Kaye**, Witness Services (Sequence Support) — the only name on C-441-KR-04 that he wrote on 29 September that he had never read.
