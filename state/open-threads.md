- **The price is the wall.** Eleven written in a book, sixty on a claim, forty-nine credits, and the sentence in his own hand: *the reason somebody else's certificate is standing up is that a woman in a first-floor room was cheap.*
- **A hand, examined by two people who do not speak.** A clerk's loop or a machine, and no name, and Arc C is where a hand is tested against a name and does not match.
- **An unsigned closing.** *No further action; the claim is within the schedule and the schedule reference is sufficient.* There is no box on the reply for a signature, because the reply is a note and not a decision, and that is the batch's institutional horror.

- **A patient who was asked nothing and told a stranger**, and whose name is in no book of his and on no document of his, and a ward sister who is on a form on the nineteenth because she said yes.
