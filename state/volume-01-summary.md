## The volume in five sentences

A night-bus dispatcher dies in a room two floors above Elias Venn's own repair shop, and the working in her threshold binds to her desk, addresses a living person, turns, and closes with a release she never performed. Over seven weeks it happens twice more in different media — a cold room on Halloway Street, then a walking stick in a public clinic — and each time the fourth act is a fabricated release laid over three acts that are genuinely somebody's own. The investigation moves through a doctor's own maintenance book, a supply chain that *answers* ward faults instead of suppressing them, a public curriculum that teaches honest people to hold an order they were given, and a rented bay in The Cut where the frames are built, until the last working is stopped by a mechanic with a chisel in nine seconds and not by the detective at all. Every document the case produces is real, signed, lodged and in the right building, and the system absorbs all of them: a certificate goes out naming a man of seventy-eight as the performer of a complete dangerous working, a door notice goes out naming an unregistered tool, and neither mentions the release that was actually performed. The volume ends with Elias Venn formally reprimanded, out of his own trade, holding a plate he has promised to give back, a notebook with nowhere to file it, and two questions asked aloud on a pavement that nobody can answer.

## The one page that matters

A magical working has a **causal order** of four acts: **anchor** (what it is bound to), **address** (what it acts upon), **turn** (the change it makes), **release** (the act that lets it finish and stop holding). Elias's faculty, the **Margin**, perceives that order and its gaps; it never perceives motive, identity, intent or the future, and it cannot tell a delivered act from a performed one. **The fourth act is a category on a Concord form, not a secret rite:** the sequence box **SEQ 4(c)**, *an act of release entered after a turn where the working contains none of its own*, whose printed concordance adds that the entry does not assert a release was performed, only that **the record is to be treated as though one was, for the purposes of this record only**.

**Why the three acts in the fourth act are the patient's.** The frame is not laid beside a working; it is laid *onto* one. Neve's bracket is a real four-act working — bound to his own stick, addressed to his own front step, turned in his own kitchen, released there — and the sleeve is bonded over the top of it, inside the ferrule. So **the anchor is genuinely his object, the address is genuinely the record that gets written about him, and the turn is genuinely his own ward closing. Only the release is not his.** The seam is an **overlap, not a gap**: on plate 1201 there is no point at which the honest order stops and the false one has not started. Every earlier frame left a fault a plate and a pen could find; this one does not, and Jules Ardent gives the reason: *you cannot build a frame that makes a man look like he did something unless the man has already done something, in the same place, in the same material, for the same reason.* What the copy wants is a record, not a person.

**Why a certificate can be true in three parts of four.** The Registry certifies a sequence, not a suspicion, and **the oldest rule in the building is that a patient's statement of his own working is not sequence material and never has been**: if it were, every man in the city who ever performed a dangerous working could certify himself out of it by saying he did not do it. So three acts are certified on the best measurement work in the city, one act nobody can separate from them because no instrument will hold two orders on one surface, and **a witness-support line that is not empty** — a withdrawn account, correctly withdrawn, cited with its footer. Bettina Haile says the whole of it in a corridor in Chapter 49: the first three acts are his, there is not one false word in them, the only thing in that certificate that is not his is the fourth, and the fourth is what this office exists to record. **The certificate is not a lie. It is a true document with a hole in it of exactly the shape the process cannot see, and Volume 02 inherits that as an argumentative problem, not a plot hole.** *(Row 12 below is the schedule structure that makes the hole; stated once, with locations, in `bible/terminology.md`.)*
