## What the volume-level rule turned out to be, in scene terms

- A **carrier** holds someone else's order. It is never the source object. A clamp holds a circuit open; it does not hold a working.
- A **source** is a person or a personal object, and it cannot be got by anyone who has not met the victim.
- A **false rehearsal** can put a different causal order into a living person's inner trace. It cannot injure anyone by itself. The harm was done by a working that was brought into the room.
- The same failure in the same joint in two different objects is a **specification**, not an accident — the pattern itself is wrong, twice.
- A working can be **delivered**: a release-shaped residue with no settling curve was made elsewhere, carried on a solid object, and seated into a slot that was already the right shape. Whether it arrived on a tool, a card, a coin or a token is not knowable at First Margin.
- A **certificate is a document with chosen words.** The public line is "a contained accident involving an unregistered ward tool." The draft certificate records a complete self-directed working by Tamsin Ro, no third-party address, no contradicting witness. Niko Senn is not named in the draft at all.
- **The fourth act is a category on a Concord form**, not a secret rite: *an act of release entered after a turn where the working contains none of its own.* That is the Chapter 9 clue to the central mystery, and it is the first time the word in Elias's twelve-year-old file turns out to be a box somebody fills in.
